WuXi XDC's Singapore BCM3 Dual-Function Line Achieves GMP Release: First Overseas Site Live, 'China + Singapore' Dual-Site Synergy

Aug 17, 2026 Source: 药明合联公众号 ↗

WuXi XDC (2268.HK) announced its Singapore BCM3 dual-function line — antibody intermediates and conjugated drug substance — has achieved GMP release, the company's first overseas GMP-released facility. With batch sizes of 200–2,000 L for intermediates and up to 2,000 L for conjugated DS, and synergy with Wuxi BCM1/BCM2, the site went from design to GMP release in just 2.5 years; the DP4 drug-product line is set for GMP release by end-August 2026.

WuXi XDC Cayman Inc. ("WuXi XDC", stock code: 2268.HK), a leading global CRDMO focused on the bioconjugate industry, announced that its BCM3 dual-function production line for antibody intermediates and conjugated drug substances at the Singapore site has successfully achieved GMP release. As the company's first overseas GMP-released facility, this milestone marks a new phase in WuXi XDC's global capacity strategy, empowering clients worldwide to accelerate commercial manufacturing with greater efficiency and flexibility in meeting diverse demands.

BCM3 features an efficient and flexible "dual-function" design, offering integrated end-to-end commercial manufacturing from antibody intermediates to conjugated drug substances. It supports batch sizes from 200 L to 2,000 L for antibody intermediates and up to 2,000 L for conjugated drug substances, with a rigorous quality control system. As a critical piece of the global manufacturing network, BCM3 will work in synergy with the BCM1 and BCM2 lines at the Wuxi site, substantially lifting capacity for both antibody intermediates and conjugated drug substances.

During construction, the team leveraged proven Wuxi-site expertise while adapting to local Singapore conditions, overcoming significant design and construction challenges in high-potency, high-toxicity zones. A flexible multi-purpose layout accommodates more project types, and optimized internal flow markedly reduced material transfer and waiting time. From facility design to GMP release, the entire process was completed in just two and a half years — once again demonstrating "WuXi Speed."

The journey: the Singapore site was announced in March 2024, reached mechanical completion in June 2025, moved through commissioning and qualification (C&Q), and achieved BCM3 GMP release in August 2026. Per the subsequent plan, the DP4 drug-product filling line is scheduled for GMP release by the end of August 2026. As the "Global Dual-Sourcing" strategy matures, the Singapore and Wuxi sites will integrate more deeply, building a comprehensive network that combines global service with local responsiveness.
